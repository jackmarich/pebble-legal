Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pebble. Or in Pebble: You › Manage subscription.

I got a new phone. Where are my tasks?
Sign in with the same Apple, Google or email account, and everything comes back. Tap Restore purchases on You if your subscription doesn't show.

How do I add the widget?
In Pebble: You › Add Pebble to your home screen.

How do I delete my account?
You › Delete account. It deletes everything for good. See the privacy policy.

Notifications aren't showing up.
Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Pebble, and You › Reminders in the app (Quiet mode pauses them all).

If you're struggling

Otto is a friendly helper, not a therapist. If you're thinking about hurting yourself or you're not safe, in the US call or text 988 any time, or 911 if you're in danger right now.
