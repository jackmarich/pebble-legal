From swirling thoughts to started, in four small steps

Pebble is built around the hardest part of any task: the first minute.

Brain Dump

Dump it all. Otto sorts it out.

Type or talk everything that's swirling around, messy is perfect. Otto turns it into tidy tasks you can add with one tap, and Undo is always there.

Help me start

Stuck? Start with one tiny step.

Every task becomes the same four small steps, so you always know what to expect:

  1. 1Open your laptop
  2. 2Open the assignment
  3. 3Write one sentence
  4. 4Write one paragraph
Focus with Otto

A calm timer, right where you are.

Every step has a focus timer, from 5 to 25 minutes. It stays on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you never lose your place when you leave the app.

Plan my day

A plan that fits your real day.

Tell Otto how much time and energy you have, and he picks a doable handful. He learns how long things really take you, and if the day drifts, he gives you a kinder plan for the rest of it.

Pebbles, not pressure

Every win drops a pebble in your jar. Spend them on Otto's closet, watch his riverbank grow, and find a postcard from his adventures each morning.

Questions

Is Pebble only for people with ADHD?

Pebble is built for ADHD brains, but anyone who finds starting harder than planning is welcome. No diagnosis needed.

How much does it cost?

Pebble is a subscription, with a free trial for new members. You'll see the exact price and trial in the app before you start, and you can cancel any time in your App Store settings.

Is Otto a therapist?

No. Otto is a friendly helper, not a therapist or a doctor, and Pebble isn't a medical device or a substitute for professional care.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Pebble is on iPhone first. Android is on the way.

How do I get help?

Visit the support page or email jackmarich1@gmail.com. A real person reads every message.
